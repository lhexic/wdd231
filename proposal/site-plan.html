Individual Project Site Plan

Lhexiverse Tech & Commercial Hub

1. Site Name

Site Name: Lhexiverse Tech & Commercial Hub

Reason for Name: The name combines "Lhex" (brand identity) with "Universe" to signify a central digital ecosystem and community platform designed to showcase technological developments, local commercial offerings, and tech services.

Domain Availability: lhexiverse.org

2. Site Purpose

The site provides a dynamic 3-page web portal designed to connect tech enthusiasts, showcase digital products, and highlight local commercial services[cite: 1]. It features an interactive hero section on the main page, a filterable digital catalog/directory for commercial vendors and tech offerings, and a resource center featuring interactive contact forms, dynamic FAQs, and weather integrations[cite: 1].

3. Scenarios

  • Scenario 1: How can I filter and search for specific commercial services or tech offerings on the platform without waiting for the page to reload[cite: 1]?
  • Scenario 2: Where can I find answers to frequently asked questions about listed products or contact the hub directly to inquire about listing my business[cite: 1]?

5. Typography

The site utilizes two Google Fonts to distinguish structure and content clearly:

Heading Font: Montserrat (Bold / Semi-bold)

Usage: Used for all section headings (h1, h2, h3) and main navigation items to ensure bold visual structure.

Body Font: Open Sans (Regular / Semi-bold)

Usage: Used for all main paragraphs, form elements, descriptions, and dynamic catalog text for optimal reading flow.
